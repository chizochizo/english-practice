📖 Word of the Day: Resilient

Pronunciation: /rɪˈzɪl.jənt/
Meaning: Able to recover quickly from hardship.

💬 "She stayed resilient through exams and passed with honors."

✏️ Micro-Lesson: "Since" vs "For"

Rule: Since marks a start point. For measures elapsed duration.

💬 "I have studied here for 2 years (duration), since 2024 (start point)."

🗣️ Idiom: "Bite the bullet"

Meaning: Facing an unpleasant situation with courage.

💬 "I hated apologizing, but I had to bite the bullet and do it."

🎯 Common Mistake: "Explain me"

Rule: Always use "Explain to me", never "Explain me".

💬 "Can you explain this to me, please?"

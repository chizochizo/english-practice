📖 Word of the Day: Resilient

Pronunciation: /rɪˈzɪl.jənt/
Meaning: Able to recover quickly from hardship or difficulty.

💬 Example: "She stayed resilient through exam stress and finished top of her class."

✏️ Micro-Lesson: "Since" vs "For"

Rule: Since marks a specific start point. For measures total duration.

💬 Example: "I have lived here for 3 years (not since 3 years). I have lived here since 2023."

🗣️ Idiom: "Bite the bullet"

Meaning: Facing an unavoidable, tough situation with courage.

💬 Example: "I was dreading the dentist appointment, but I just bit the bullet and went."

🎯 Common Mistake: "Explain me"

Rule: Explain requires the preposition to when naming the listener.

💬 Example: Say "Can you explain this to me?" (Never say "Explain me this").
